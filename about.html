About Midnight Calm

Midnight Calm is a focus & sound experience designed for night-time workers and students. The platform helps users either stay focused or relax by recommending suitable background sounds.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support productivity and mental calm during late hours. Midnight Calm helps users quickly find the right background sound for focus or sleep without distractions or complex settings.

What We Offer

  • Curated rain, white noise, and ambient sound recommendations
  • A minimal interface designed for deep focus
  • Calm soundscapes to support relaxation and sleep

Design Principles

Midnight Calm follows a dark, night-inspired visual design with high readability, consistent spacing, and minimal UI components. The website is fully responsive and optimized for both mobile and desktop devices.

Team

This project was developed as part of an academic web development course. All textual and visual content was produced by the project team. Third-party libraries such as fonts and icons are used in accordance with their licenses.
